# Saeideh Mansouri

**Telecommunications Engineer | Wireless Networks | Data Analytics & Machine Learning**

M.Sc. in Communications Engineering, Aalto University · Espoo, Finland

I work at the intersection of wireless communications, network performance engineering, and data-driven analysis. My background combines research in wireless propagation and railway communications with industry experience in RAN/OSS analytics, Python automation, and SQL-based telecom reporting.

## Areas of focus

- **Wireless systems research:** radio propagation, ray tracing, mobile network simulation, and digital-twin modeling with Wireless InSite and Sionna RT.
- **RAN performance engineering:** KPI analysis, performance evaluation, and troubleshooting across 2G, 3G, and LTE networks.
- **Machine learning for networks:** regression, traffic-pattern clustering, and data-driven estimation of network performance indicators.
- **Telecom data engineering:** multi-vendor data integration, ETL workflows, SQL analysis, and automated technical reporting.
